Who We Are

Welcome to JCM Group of Schools, where we are committed to providing a nurturing and enriching educational environment. Our first branch, located in Mbundane, opened its doors in January 2025, offering classes from ECD to Grade 5. We're excited to announce that we are now enrolling students up to Grade 6! Beyond a solid academic foundation, students enjoy a variety of activities including swimming, chess, and foreign language.

We have also opened JCM Academy in Esigodini! This new branch will eventually offer education from ECD up to Form 4. However, for the initial intake, we are actively enrolling students from ECD to Grade 6.

OUR MISSION STATEMENT

JCM Primary School is an institution which strives to cultivate and nature learners with baobab mind to produce resilient adaptable and innovative citizens.
JCM Primary School envisages to empower learners with deep rooted knowledge, skills , values and wisdom while venturing out into a world on innovation and excellence , through a disciplined learning which fosters creativity , critical thinking and compassion in learners.
