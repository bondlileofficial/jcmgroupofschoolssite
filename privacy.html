1. Scope

This policy applies to the JCM school management application operated by JCM Group of Schools. The application supports school administration, including learner records, admissions, attendance, fees and payments, expenses, fundraising, examinations, communication, and staff administration.

The school is responsible for deciding what school information is entered into the application and who is authorized to use it.

2. Information collected

Depending on how you use the application, information may include:

  • Account information: name, email address, username, school role, campus, and class or grade.
  • School records: learner and guardian names and contact details, class and enrolment details, attendance, examination information, fee and payment records, fundraising and expense entries, messages, and staff records entered by the school.
  • Sign-in information: authentication identifiers and session information managed through Supabase Auth, or basic identity details returned when you choose Google sign-in.
  • Technical information: information needed to deliver and protect the service, which may be processed by the hosting and authentication providers.

The application does not ask for access to Gmail, Google Drive, Google Contacts, or other Google services.

3. Google sign-in

If you choose “Continue with Google,” Google provides an authentication credential so Supabase Auth can verify your identity and sign you in. The application receives basic account information such as your email address and profile name, subject to your Google account settings.

Google sign-in is used only to authenticate your application account. JCM does not use this sign-in to read or modify your Google content. Google's handling of information is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

4. How information is used

JCM uses information to authenticate users, operate the school portal, maintain school and learner records, provide role-specific application features, communicate about school administration, protect the service, respond to requests, and meet applicable legal or recordkeeping obligations.

Information is not used to serve advertising or to build advertising profiles.

5. When information is shared

Information may be available to JCM personnel who need it for legitimate school and administrative purposes. The application also relies on service providers to operate:

  • Supabase provides authentication and, when configured, the application database.
  • Netlify hosts the application and its configuration function.
  • Google provides Google sign-in when you select that option.

These providers process information to provide their services under their own terms and privacy policies. JCM does not sell personal information. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or to protect the rights, safety, and security of the school community.

6. Storage and retention

When the application is correctly configured, shared school records are stored in the JCM Supabase project. The browser may also retain a local copy of application state and preferences so the interface can work; browser-local changes do not become shared records unless the application reports that the Supabase save succeeded. Signing out does not necessarily erase browser storage. Clear the browser's site data on a shared device after use.

JCM retains school and account information for as long as it is needed to operate the application, meet school recordkeeping responsibilities, resolve disputes, or comply with legal obligations. Retention periods may differ by record type.

7. Your choices and rights

You may ask JCM to access, correct, or delete information associated with your account, subject to school recordkeeping and legal requirements. Contact the school using the details below. You can also remove JCM's access to your Google account from your Google Account settings; this does not automatically delete school records or your JCM account.

8. Children's information

The application may contain information about learners because it is used for school administration. Learner information should be entered and managed by authorized school personnel. Account requests for learners should be made or approved by the school and, where appropriate, a parent or guardian.

9. Security

JCM and its service providers use administrative and technical measures intended to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Users should keep their sign-in details private, sign out on shared devices, and report suspected account or data issues promptly.

Access to records depends on the application's configuration and the accounts authorized by the school. Please contact JCM if you believe information is available to someone who should not have access.
